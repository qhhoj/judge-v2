A case that ends TLE on its own, under the hard cap, is judged once more while no other run is on the host, and that result is the case's (COR-48: CPU time under load ran up to x1.46 for cache-bound code). Every isolate run, `selftest`'s too, holds a host-wide run gate shared; the re-run holds it exclusive. At most `limits.tle_reruns` (default 2, 0 turns them off) re-runs per submission may confirm a TLE; one that flips the verdict is free. A contestant run is now killed at max(TL + 0.5 s, 2 x TL) of CPU instead of at the first tick past the limit (`--extra-time` is absolute), so a TLE reports its real time; a run past the time limit is TLE even if the cgroup then OOM-killed it. Limits reach isolate as whole milliseconds plus half a millisecond (isolate truncates), and times are compared in whole milliseconds. A whole case, not only its isolate runs, holds the gate shared. The daemon and `check-config` refuse a `cache_dir` that group or others can reach (the lock files live there, 0600).
